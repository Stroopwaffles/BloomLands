# Bloom Lands — Content Document

*Companion to `design.md`. The design doc defines the systems; this doc defines the content that fills them: regions, plants, characters, recipes, items.*
*Status: Brainstorming. Last updated: 2026-11-13.*

All numbers here (toughness, rarity) are **starting points for balancing**, not final values.

**Rarity scale:** Common · Uncommon · Rare · Very rare
**Toughness:** clicks needed at base click power.

---

## Regions

### Region 1: Meadow (starting region)

*A sunny, open field of tall grass and wildflowers. Gentle and welcoming: the place where the player's court begins.*

**Palette:** sunny yellows, fresh greens, sky blue.

#### Sublocations

| Sublocation | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Thimblewick** | Settlement | A village of field mice living in burrows and old thimbles under the roots of a hawthorn. |
| **Buttercup Rise** | Harvesting spot | A gentle sunny slope. The first spot the player ever visits; easy plants only. |
| **Dandelion Drift** | Harvesting spot | A breezy hollow where dandelion seeds float. Slightly tougher plants and the Meadow's rare finds. |
| **Sunstone Hill** | Spirit's place | A warm flat stone at the top of the meadow where the spirit rests. |

#### Plants and Resources

| Plant | Where | Toughness | Rarity | Gives (raw material) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clover | Buttercup Rise | 1 | Common | Clover leaves |
| Daisy | Buttercup Rise | 2 | Common | Daisy petals |
| Buttercup | Buttercup Rise | 2 | Common | Buttercup petals |
| Dandelion | Dandelion Drift | 3 | Common | Dandelion fluff |
| Wild strawberry | Dandelion Drift | 4 | Uncommon | Wild berries |
| Morning dew | Both (mornings / after rain) | 1 | Uncommon | Dewdrops |
| Four-leaf clover | Buttercup Rise | 1 | Rare | Lucky leaf |

#### Spirit of the Land

**Sorrel, the Meadow's Breath:** a gentle, sleepy spirit shaped like a swirl of grass seed and sunlight. Wakes a little more as bloom rises.

#### Characters

| Character | Where | Storyline (short) | Curio reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pip Thistledown** | Thimblewick | An elderly field-mouse mapmaker whose maps blew away in a storm. The player helps recover the pieces, which also teaches travel. | **Pip's Compass**: travel time slightly reduced |
| **Marigold** | Thimblewick | A young mouse baker who wants to bake the perfect strawberry tart for the village festival. | **Festival Ribbon**: trader requests pay slightly more Glimmers |

#### Challenges

- **Large creature:** **Rabbit**, munches through a harvesting spot; shooed by tapping, or calmed with a spell.
- **Pests:** aphids on daisies and buttercups.
- **Pirate crew:** **The Dandelion Drifters**, a crew who ride dandelion seeds and swoop down on gatherers. Easiest crew; introduces pirates.
- **Weather:**
  - **Sunshine:** plants regrow faster.
  - **Breezy:** flights shorter heading home, longer heading out (*or random*).
  - **Light rain:** slower flights, but morning dew is plentiful.

#### Mount

**Bumblebee:** starter mount; extra carrying capacity in the Meadow. *Favorite food for befriending:* clover nectar.

#### Secret Area

**The Fairy Ring:** a circle of mushrooms that only appears after the player performs a ritual at dawn.
- **Curio:** **Pocket Sundial**: potion durations slightly longer.
- **Exclusive plant:** Ringcap mushroom (rare).
- **Unique outfit:** **Dewdrop Gown / Dewdrop Cloak** (headpiece of dew beads).

#### Trinkets found here

Lost buttons, a glass bead, a thimble.

## Pirate Captain

**Captain Mallow Brambleshanks:** a flamboyant pirate fairy with a patched petal coat and an enormous feathered hat.

- **Backstory:** her home grove withered long ago, and she decided the land could never be healed, so she takes whatever she can find.
- **Arc:** as the player heals region after region and wins over her crews, she grows curious, then doubtful, then hopeful. Her storyline runs across the whole game, with a chapter unlocked after each crew is won over.
- **Resolution:** after the last crew (the Stormchasers) is won over, she joins the player's court. She appears in the ending celebration.

### Pirate crews (summary)

| Region | Crew | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Meadow | The Dandelion Drifters | Ride dandelion seeds; easiest crew |
| Forest | The Bramble Corsairs | Hide in thorn thickets |
| Swamp | The Bog Buccaneers | Sail leaf boats |
| Blossom Orchard | The Nectar Nabbers | Steal honey and pollen |
| Caves | The Stalactite Smugglers | Drop from the ceiling |
| Moonlit Glade | The Moonshadow Crew | Move only in shadows |
| Sky Isles | The Stormchasers | The captain's elite crew; hardest |

Each crew has a small storyline. Winning a crew over clears its camp for the run and lets the player recruit reformed pirates as helpers.

## Recipe Tree

Every recipe below is **unlocked through research** (see design doc 3.6). Quantities are left for balancing; recipes list **which** inputs, not how many.

**The flow:**

```
Raw materials ──(refine)──> Refined materials ──(combine)──> Components ──> Potions (brewed)
                                                                       └──> Spells (cast cost)
                                                                       └──> Rituals (performed cost)
```

Potions are brewed from components. **Spells and rituals are not crafted**: they are researched once, and each cast or performance **consumes components**.

### Stations (at home)

| Station | Does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mortar** | Grind → pastes, dusts, flours, powders | |
| **Press** | Press → juices, oils, syrups | |
| **Drying Rack** | Dry → teas, dried herbs | |
| **Spinning Wheel** | Spin → threads, silks | |
| **Cauldron** | Combine refined materials → components; brew components → potions | Slots upgradeable (design doc 3.4) |
| **Ritual Circle** | Perform rituals | |

Each station is **unlocked by a lodging upgrade**, matching how helpers are unlocked. **Brewer helpers** can run stations automatically.

### Material Tags

To keep the tree manageable, many basic recipes accept **any** raw material with the right **tag**. Region-specific plants feed region-specific recipes.

| Tag | Raw materials |
|---|---|
| **Petals** | Daisy, buttercup, marigold, apple, cherry, quince, primrose, moonpetal, skybell, sunpetal, lily, orchid, ghost, midnight petals |
| **Leaves** | Clover leaves, fern fronds, moss tufts, myrtle leaves, bee balm leaves, silver fronds, cave moss, starmoss, rainbow moss |
| **Caps** | Mushroom caps, red caps, golden caps, glowcap spores, star spores |
| **Fruit** | Wild berries, bilberries, crabapples, acorns |
| **Fluff** | Dandelion fluff, cattail fluff, thistledown, cloud fluff |
| **Stalks** | Reed stalks, root fibers |

Remaining raws (dewdrops, duckweed, honey, golden pollen, lucky leaf, crystal shards, geode crystals, moonlight, storm essence, aurora petals, jasmine blossoms) are **special** and used by name.

## Relics

Relics are **permanent** (kept through prestige). Each spirit gives **one relic per run**, in order, when its requirements are met: relic I in run 1, relic II in run 2, and so on through relic V in run 5.

**How the three bonus types differ:**
- **Curios:** small bonuses for the current run only.
- **Relics:** permanent, region-themed, often *change how something works* rather than adding a percentage.
- **Root Song upgrades:** permanent, broad stat boosts the player chooses to buy.

**Every spirit's five relics follow the same pattern**, so players learn what to expect:

| # | Pattern | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Head start** | The region's bloom starts one level higher each run |
| II | **Truce** | The region's large creature becomes friendly and never blocks spots |
| III | **Kinship** | The region's mount arrives already tamed each run (once the region is unlocked) |
| IV | **Specialty** | A unique change to how the region works |
| V | **Heart** | A powerful game-wide bonus in the spirit's theme |

## Asset List

Every piece of art the game needs, compiled from both documents. All art is **SVG** in the soft storybook style (design doc Section 7).

### Conventions

- **Stable IDs:** every asset has a kebab-case ID, `category-name`, e.g. `plant-clover`, `mount-frog`, `potion-lucky-dew`. Code refers only to IDs, so any asset can be replaced later without touching game logic.
- **Recolor, don't redraw:** colors (skin, hair, outfits, bloom stages, day/night) are applied with CSS. One drawing serves many variants.
- **Templates:** families of similar items share one base drawing with a color or symbol change (see "Template" notes below). This cuts the drawing workload a lot.
- **Animation:** done with CSS on parts of the SVG (wings, leaves, glows), so most assets need only one drawing, built with named, separate parts.
- **Priority:** **P1** = needed for a first playable build (Meadow + home + core UI). **P2** = everything else.
